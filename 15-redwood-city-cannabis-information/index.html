Redwood City Cannabis Delivery, Storefront History, and Receipt Basics

Redwood City’s approach to commercial cannabis has progressed through carefully planned municipal stages. What began in 2018 as a limited framework for delivery hubs and plant nurseries expanded in 2020 to permit storefront dispensaries, culminating in authorization for up to six retail permits by 2022. Understanding this timeline, along with the city’s 4% local cannabis business tax, helps consumers make sense of their options and audit their purchase receipts.

Storefronts vs. Delivery Depots: What to Expect

  • Storefront Dispensaries: Retail establishments with open display areas, reception desks, and in-person consultations with budtenders.
  • Delivery-Only Centers: Industrial facilities that fulfill online orders for home delivery; they are strictly closed to walk-in traffic.
  • A permit authorization cap (such as up to six permits) represents a municipal limit, not an audited count of open businesses. Check current business records before traveling to a physical address.

Redwood City’s 4% Local Cannabis Business Tax

Redwood City assesses a 4% local gross-receipts tax on retail cannabis operations.

  • Who Pays: The tax applies to all retail cannabis transactions occurring within Redwood City, whether purchased at an in-city storefront or delivered by an outside dispensary.
  • How It Appears on Receipts: Dispensaries may include the 4% tax within base product prices or display it as a separate line item at checkout.
  • Not the Only Tax: This municipal business tax is separate from California's state cannabis excise tax and standard state and local sales taxes.

Account Credits vs. True Cash Refunds

If an order issue occurs, dispensaries often offer store credit rather than a direct refund:

  • Store Credit: Digital balance retained in your dispensary account toward future purchases; cannot be redeemed for cash.
  • Payment Refund: A direct return of funds to your original payment method.
  • Audit Pending vs. Settled Charges: If an order is canceled or modified, check your mobile banking app to confirm whether a pending authorization was reversed or a refund was processed.
