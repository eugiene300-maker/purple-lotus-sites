Cannabis Prices and Rewards: Understand the Numbers on the Page

Browsing a dispensary menu or looking at a cannabis receipt can be baffling. A product listed for $40 online can easily total $55 or more at checkout after local taxes, state excise fees, and delivery surcharges are applied. Meanwhile, rewards programs promise points and discounts that work differently in practice than they appear on promotional banners. This guide breaks down the arithmetic behind cannabis pricing, recent tax changes, and loyalty programs.

California’s 2025 Cannabis Excise Tax Fluctuation

If you compare purchase receipts from different times in 2025, you might notice varying tax rates:

  • Prior to July 1, 2025: California cannabis excise tax was assessed at 15%.
  • July 1, 2025 – September 30, 2025: The California Department of Tax and Fee Administration (CDTFA) adjusted the rate upward to 19%.
  • October 1, 2025 and Onward: The excise tax returned to the baseline 15% rate.
  • A mid-2025 receipt reflecting a 19% excise tax was legally correct for that window and does not represent a dispensary billing error.

Understanding Percentage Math: The "Base" Matters

A discount or tax percentage is only as meaningful as the underlying number it applies to:

  • A "10% off" discount applied to a $40 product saves you $4.00.
  • A "10% off" discount applied to an $80 product saves you $8.00.
  • Verify whether a store promotion applies to your entire cart or only to full-priced items before taxes are calculated.

Decoding Rewards Programs: Points Are Not Cash

Dispensaries frequently promote loyalty rewards programs:

  • Points Conversion Formulas: Points do not equal dollars. For example, a program might award 1 point per $1 spent, requiring 100 points to earn a $5 store credit. In that scenario, each point is worth 5 cents.
  • Expiration Dates: Many loyalty programs include expiration clauses that forfeit unused points after 6 or 12 months of inactivity.
  • Restrictions: Loyalty points often cannot be combined with existing daily deals or applied toward delivery fees.

How to Dispute a Questionable Charge

If you notice an unexpected charge on your receipt:

  1. Identify the Exact Line Item: Check whether the issue is an unrecognized service fee, a missing discount, or a tax miscalculation.
  2. Contact Customer Support with Invoice Data: Provide your order number, date, and receipt photograph.
  3. State Your Request Factual and Direct: Say: "My order invoice dated [Date] reflects a subtotal of $50, but the advertised 20% discount code [Code] was not deducted prior to tax calculation. Please review the attached receipt and apply the $10 credit/refund".
