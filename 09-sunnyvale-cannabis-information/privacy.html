Privacy policy

Your visit.
Your information.

This is a reading-only website. You do not need an account, and the pages do not ask you to submit personal information.

Information collected by these pages

The site has no registration, contact form, newsletter form, checkout, or comment system. Its pages do not collect names, email addresses, payment details, or uploaded documents. They do not request your device location.

Cookies, analytics, and browser storage

The site code does not set cookies, store information in your browser, or include analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or behavioral tracking tools. Images, styles, and icons are served with the site rather than loaded from embedded social-media services.

Hosting and routine access logs

Your browser sends technical information when requesting any webpage. The hosting provider may process an IP address, request time, page address, browser information, and other connection details to deliver the site, maintain security, or diagnose errors. These pages do not specify the hosting provider’s retention period or replace its privacy policy.

Links to other websites

The guide links to external references and company information. Following a link takes you to a separate website with its own privacy practices. Depending on your browser settings, that website may receive information about the page you came from. Review its policy before sharing personal information there.

Your choices

You can read every page without signing in. Your browser settings let you control cookies, referrer information, and other privacy preferences. Blocking external websites does not prevent you from reading the locally hosted guide.

Audience and future changes

The guide is intended for adults. It does not knowingly solicit personal information from children or operate a user-profile system. If forms, analytics, advertising, or other data-collection features are introduced, this policy should be updated to describe them before they are enabled.
