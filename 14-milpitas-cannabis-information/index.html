Milpitas Cannabis Delivery: Medicinal Access and Local Registration

Milpitas maintains a distinct, carefully structured approach to cannabis within its borders. In 2019, the city passed a comprehensive ordinance banning commercial cannabis storefronts, yet its municipal code provides an established process through the Milpitas Police Department allowing licensed outside businesses to deliver medicinal cannabis directly to qualified patients. This guide explains how this dual framework operates, how patient protections apply, and how to safeguard your privacy.

Milpitas Ordinance 298 vs. Medicinal Delivery

In January 2019, the Milpitas City Council adopted Ordinance 298:

  • The Commercial Ban: Prohibits physical cannabis dispensaries, commercial cultivation facilities, manufacturing labs, and testing sites within Milpitas city limits.
  • The Medicinal Accommodation: Recognizes the healthcare needs of local residents by establishing an administrative registration process with the Milpitas Police Department.
  • Business-Facing Registration: Licensed cannabis retailers from neighboring jurisdictions (such as San Jose) register their businesses and delivery drivers with the city to lawfully serve qualified patients and primary caregivers.

How State Law (SB 1186) Strengthens Patient Access

California’s Senate Bill 1186 (effective January 1, 2024) limits the ability of local governments to block medicinal cannabis deliveries to qualified patients.

  • Even in cities with historical commercial prohibitions, local governments cannot prevent licensed dispensaries from delivering medicinal cannabis to patients holding a valid physician recommendation or state MMIC.
  • Milpitas’s registration framework fits alongside this state requirement, ensuring eligible patients maintain reliable access to necessary therapies.

Protecting Your Medical and Personal Information

Ordering medicinal cannabis involves sensitive documentation:

  • What Dispensaries Must Verify: Under state law, retailers must verify your age (government photo ID) and confirm your medical eligibility (written doctor's recommendation or official state MMIC).
  • Keep Documents Off Public Channels: Never submit photos of your medical recommendation or driver's license via social media messages, public review pages, or unverified contact forms.
  • Audit Dispensary Privacy Disclosures: Ensure the dispensary's ordering portal uses secure encryption to store customer profiles and medical records.
  • Device Management: If you use a shared home computer or tablet, log out of dispensary accounts and clear order confirmation downloads from your browser history.

Safe and Secure Storage in Shared Households

Whether consuming cannabis for medicinal or adult-use purposes, responsible storage is critical:

  • Original Packaging: Keep all products in their original child-resistant containers with labels intact.
  • Locked Storage: Use a lockbox or locked medicine cabinet to prevent accidental access by children, roommates, or visitors.
  • Avoid Food Mix-Ups: Never store infused edibles (gummies, chocolates, beverages) alongside everyday groceries or snacks in the refrigerator.
