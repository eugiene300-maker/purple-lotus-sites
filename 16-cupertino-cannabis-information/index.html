Cupertino Cannabis Delivery, Shared Homes, and Privacy

In Cupertino, residential cannabis consumption requires careful attention to the city's strict smoke-free multiunit housing laws. While adult residents can legally order cannabis delivery to their private residences under California law, Cupertino’s municipal code regulates smoking and vaping across multiunit properties, including duplexes, townhomes, and attached single-family residences. This guide explains Cupertino's 30-foot buffer rules, property definitions, and everyday privacy best practices.

Cupertino’s Broad Definition of "Multiunit Housing"

In October 2021, the City of Cupertino expanded its smoke-free housing regulations:

  • What Qualifies as Multiunit Housing: Under Cupertino code, a multiunit residence includes any building with two or more attached units. This covers apartment complexes, condominiums, townhomes, and duplexes sharing a common wall, floor, or ceiling.
  • Private Living Spaces Included: The smoke-free requirement applies inside private living units, on attached balconies, decks, and patios, and in shared courtyards.
  • Vaping is Regulated Equally: The municipal code treats electronic vaporizing devices identical to combustible cannabis and tobacco.

The 30-Foot Buffer Rule: Where Does It Apply?

Cupertino enforces a mandatory 30-foot smoke-free buffer around building openings:

  • You cannot smoke or vape within 30 feet of any operable door, window, or ventilation intake duct of public and multiunit residential buildings.
  • In dense townhome or condominium communities, finding an outdoor location that is a full 30 feet away from every neighboring window is often difficult.
  • Standing on an outdoor patio does not clear you from the regulation if you are within 30 feet of a neighbor's window.

Odorless Alternatives for Attached Living

For Cupertino residents living in attached homes, switching to non-smoked delivery formats avoids lease violations, HOA disputes, and second-hand smoke complaints:

  • Tinctures & Sublingual Drops: Absorbed under the tongue for rapid, smoke-free effects.
  • Gummies, Chocolates & Beverages: Digestible edibles offering controlled milligram doses without aroma.
  • Capsules & Tablets: Familiar pill formats with precise, measured cannabinoid contents.
  • Topicals: Balms and lotions applied directly to the skin for targeted relief.

Protecting Privacy in Shared Homes and Online

Ordering cannabis delivery requires sharing personal information:

  • Manage Lock-Screen Notifications: Delivery dispatchers send automated SMS updates when drivers are en route. If your phone or tablet is visible to housemates, turn off message previews on your lock screen.
  • Secure Delivery Drop-Off: Meet your driver at the door with your physical ID ready. Do not share exterior building access codes or gate PINs in delivery delivery notes.
  • Check Checkout Privacy Notices: Verify whether an online dispensary stores your customer records in-house or shares data with third-party marketing networks.

Household Storage Safety

  • Keep Products Locked: Store cannabis in a locked box or secure cabinet out of sight and reach of children, roommates, and houseguests.
  • Retain Original Packaging: Never transfer edibles into plain jars, candy dishes, or snack containers where they can be mistaken for ordinary food.
  • Pet Precautions: Keep infused treats stored securely; dogs and cats are highly sensitive to THC and require immediate veterinary care if accidentally exposed.
