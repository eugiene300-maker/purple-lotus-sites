Common Questions and Plain-Language Answers

1. Where are Purple Lotus’s two San Jose locations situated?

  • Downtown San Jose: 66 W Santa Clara St, San Jose, CA 95113.
  • Note: West Santa Clara Street is a major downtown San Jose street, not the separate City of Santa Clara.

2. What is the legal difference between adult-use and medicinal cannabis?

  • Adult-Use (Recreational): Available to adults 21 and older who show a valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID (driver’s license, state ID, or passport).
  • Medicinal: Available to California residents 18 and older who hold an official written recommendation from a licensed physician or a state Medical Marijuana Identification Card (MMIC).

3. Is a licensed cannabis dispensary the same as a pharmacy?

No. Dispensaries are licensed retail businesses authorized to sell commercial cannabis under California cannabis laws. They are not pharmacies, and budtenders are not medical doctors or registered pharmacists. Retail staff can explain product formats, but they cannot diagnose health conditions or prescribe treatments.

4. What is the difference between total package milligrams and a serving amount?

The total package number indicates the cannabinoid mass across the entire product (e.g., 100 mg THC in a box of gummies), while the serving amount indicates the dose per individual piece (e.g., 10 mg THC per single gummy). Always base your consumption on the serving size, not the total package number.

5. Can I compare flower percentages directly with edible milligrams?

No. Percentages measure relative concentration by weight in raw plant matter, whereas milligrams measure exact mass in an edible or tincture. Inhaled cannabis passes immediately into the bloodstream via the lungs, while ingested edibles are processed by the liver into 11-hydroxy-THC, which produces a much stronger, longer-lasting effect.

7. Do "indica," "sativa," and "hybrid" guarantee how a product will feel?

No. These terms describe the botanical growth habits and lineage of the plant. Individual effects depend on total cannabinoid dosage, terpene profiles, personal metabolism, tolerance, and your current environment.

8. Does mandatory lab testing guarantee a product is completely safe?

No. Passing state testing confirms that a sample from that batch met California standards for potency and was free from actionable levels of pesticides, heavy metals, residual solvents, and mold. It does not eliminate the risk of impairment, prevent medication interactions, or guarantee an individual won't feel adverse side effects.

9. How do I verify whether my product is included in a state safety recall?

Check the product package for the track-and-trace UID, batch code, and lot number. Compare these exact numbers against the safety notices published on the California Department of Cannabis Control (DCC) Recalls page. If the numbers match, discontinue use and follow the recall return instructions.

10. Can I consume cannabis on-site at a dispensary or in a nearby parking lot?

No. California law strictly prohibits public cannabis consumption. Consuming in parking lots, on sidewalks, in vehicles, or in public parks is illegal. Cannabis must be transported sealed and consumed on private property.

11. Why do neighboring Bay Area cities have different cannabis rules?

While California sets baseline regulations for the state, Proposition 64 grants local cities and counties the authority to create their own commercial zoning and business rules. A city like San Jose allows both storefronts and delivery, Mountain View limits permits to delivery hubs, and Palo Alto bans storefronts while permitting outside delivery.

12. What records should I save if my purchase receipt looks incorrect?

Retain your itemized paper or digital receipt, take clear photos of the product and its batch labels, and keep a copy of your bank transaction record. Contact dispensary support with your order number and invoice to request a correction or credit.

13. Who regulates cannabis dispensaries in the City of San Jose?

San Jose dispensaries are regulated locally by the San Jose Police Department Division of Cannabis Regulation (DCR):

  • Phone: 408-537-1420
  • Email: dcr@sanjoseca.gov

14. How should I protect my personal data when ordering cannabis?

Verify that you are ordering through an encrypted dispensary portal. Adjust your phone's notification settings to turn off lock-screen text previews, and keep identity documents off public review sites and unverified messages.
