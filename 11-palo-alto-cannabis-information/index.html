Palo Alto Cannabis Delivery: City Rules and Stanford’s Separate Policy

Residents living in Palo Alto must navigate two distinct sets of regulations: the municipal code governing the city, and the strict institutional policies governing Stanford University. While Palo Alto's municipal code prohibits physical cannabis storefronts, it includes an explicit exception that allows legal deliveries to private residences from licensed outside dispensaries. However, crossing onto Stanford property introduces an entirely separate set of rules. This guide clarifies these boundaries.

Palo Alto Municipal Code Section 18.42.150 Explained

Palo Alto's zoning code takes a specific approach to commercial cannabis:

  • No Physical Commercial Storefronts: The city prohibits commercial dispensaries, cultivation sites, and cannabis testing facilities within city limits.
  • The Outside Delivery Exception: Municipal Code Section 18.42.150 explicitly permits the delivery of cannabis to private addresses from licensed cannabis businesses located outside the city, in full alignment with California Business and Professions Code Section 26090.
  • What This Means for You: Palo Alto residents can legally order delivery to their homes from licensed dispensaries located in neighboring cities like San Jose.

Stanford University: A Completely Separate Policy

While the City of Palo Alto permits residential delivery, Stanford University enforces its own alcohol and drug policy across all campus grounds:

  • Campus-Wide Cannabis Ban: Stanford strictly prohibits the possession, use, storage, sale, cultivation, manufacture, and delivery of cannabis anywhere on university property.
  • Federal Law Compliance: Because Stanford receives federal research grants and funding, it must comply with federal laws, including the Drug-Free Schools and Communities Act. Under federal law, cannabis remains a Schedule I controlled substance.
  • Areas Subject to the Ban: The prohibition covers all undergraduate and graduate student housing, campus dorms, faculty housing on campus grounds, administrative buildings, university libraries, athletic facilities, and campus parking lots.
  • Do Not Order Delivery to Campus: Licensed delivery drivers cannot fulfill orders on Stanford campus property; doing so violates university policy and puts student standing at risk.

Off-Campus Rented Housing and Workplaces

  • Private Leases in Palo Alto: Landlords and property managers in Palo Alto have the legal authority to ban smoking and vaping on their properties. Always review your lease terms.
  • Non-Combustible Products: For apartment renters seeking to avoid lease violations, non-smoked formats such as tinctures, capsules, and gummies provide a discreet alternative.
  • Workplace Policies: Commercial office parks throughout Palo Alto and Stanford Research Park maintain independent drug-free workplace rules.

Digital Privacy When Ordering Delivery

  • When ordering delivery to a private Palo Alto residence, you will need to submit a government photo ID to verify age.
  • Confirm you are placing orders through licensed dispensaries that use encrypted, secure checkout systems.
  • Keep your purchase records in a private email account or secure folder to prevent notifications from displaying on shared home computers or family devices.
